**Charlotte, NC – May 26, 2026**


Sentinel Grove Partners today announced the successful completion to fund Boulangerie
Copain, a state-of-the-art commercial bakery by veteran restaurateur Chef Jim Noble. The
10,000-square-foot artisanal bread production facility, located at 200 W. 24th St. in Charlotte,
will drastically expand baking capacity and serve as the dedicated supplier for the growing
Noble Food & Pursuits restaurant group.


This strategic investment will enable the bakery to produce ten times its current capacity,
utilizing top-tier equipment such as French-made Bongard ovens and sourcing locally milled
organic flour to craft naturally fermented loaves from a 30-year-old sourdough starter. The
facility will exclusively supply highly sought-after baked goods ranging from Bossy Beulah’s
chicken sandwich buns to the signature baguettes at Rooster’s Wood-Fired Kitchen, and French
inspired pastries at Copain. Boulangerie Copain is slated to oﬃcially open its doors in June of
this year.

and with Boulangerie Copain, we are finally bringing that dream to life on a grand scale," said
Chef Jim Noble. "Having a strategic partner like Sentinel Grove Partners who truly understands
our vision allows us to focus on what we do best - baking excellent bread. With our new steam-
injected ovens and dedicated cooling rooms, we are going to take the bread at every single one
of our restaurants up a major notch."


**About Sentinel Grove Partners**
Sentinel Grove Partners is a boutique investment firm that provides curated access to high-
caliber commercial real estate, emerging private companies, and innovative fixed-income
instruments. Driven by the philosophy of "Growth Protected," the firm focuses on reducing
barriers and volatility for accredited investors by assembling bespoke private investment
opportunities designed for long-term wealth creation.


**About Noble Food & Pursuits**
Founded by Chef Jim Noble, Noble Food & Pursuits is a premier restaurant and hospitality group
based in Charlotte, North Carolina. The group operates a portfolio of beloved local concepts,
including Rooster’s Wood-Fired Kitchen, Noble Smoke, Bossy Beulah’s, The Jimmy, and Copain.
Driven by a passion for scratch-made, locally sourced ingredients and exceptional hospitality,
Noble Food & Pursuits continues to define the culinary landscape of the Carolinas.
